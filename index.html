NGCI Technical Execution

  • Timing: Add in the final minutes of the boil or during whirlpool to prevent early-boil density from inhibiting hop utilisation.
  • Purpose: Ensures high fermentability and a dry finish, countering the "pudding effect" inherent in high-starch millet/buckwheat mashes.
  • pH Check: Ensure knockout pH is targeted at 5.1. Maintaining proper pH prevents the extraction of grainy husk tannins.
